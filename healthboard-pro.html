About This Role

Memorial Hermann is seeking an experienced Travel ICU Registered Nurse for our 24-bed Medical Intensive Care Unit. You'll be caring for critically ill patients including post-surgical, sepsis, and multi-organ failure cases in a Level I Trauma Center environment.

Requirements
  • Active Texas RN license (or Compact license)
  • Minimum 2 years recent ICU/Critical Care experience
  • ACLS and BLS certifications (current)
  • CCRN preferred but not required
  • Experience with ventilator management, hemodynamic monitoring, and drip titration
  • Epic EMR experience preferred
Benefits Package
  • $62/hr base rate (tax-free stipend eligible)
  • $2,400/week housing stipend + M&IE
  • Medical, dental, vision insurance (Day 1)
  • 401(k) with company match
  • $500 completion bonus at 13 weeks
  • Free CEU access through platform
  • 24/7 support from dedicated recruiter
About Memorial Hermann

Memorial Hermann Health System is one of Texas's largest not-for-profit health systems with 17 hospitals. Rated 4.8/5 by travel nurses for communication, facilities, and support. Magnet® recognized for nursing excellence.
