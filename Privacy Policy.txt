MedHunt (“MedHunt,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy of our users and the individuals whose professional information may be processed through our service.
This Privacy Policy explains how MedHunt collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects information when users access or use the MedHunt Chrome extension, website, platform, and related services.
MedHunt is designed to help recruiters and staffing professionals find, identify, match, and connect with potential candidates for legitimate recruiting and staffing purposes.
1. Information We May Process
MedHunt may process information provided by users or information available through supported recruiting websites and authorized data sources.
Candidate and Professional Information
The information processed may include:
First and last name
Professional title
Occupation or specialty
Employer or work history
Education information
City, state, or other general location information
Professional profile information
Publicly or professionally available email addresses
Phone numbers
Resume information
Skills, certifications, and professional qualifications
Candidate profile URLs or identifiers
MedHunt is intended to process professional and recruiting-related information.
User Information
When users access MedHunt, we may process limited information such as:
Name
Business email address
Organization or company name
Account information
Extension settings and preferences
Search or workflow information necessary to operate the service
Website Content
When a user initiates a MedHunt workflow on a supported recruiting website, the extension may access relevant content displayed on that webpage in order to identify candidate information, compare profiles, match candidates, or perform other recruiting-related functions requested by the user.
MedHunt does not use website access to monitor a user's unrelated browsing activity.
2. How We Use Information
We use information only for MedHunt's recruiting and candidate-sourcing functionality.
Information may be used to:
Search for potential candidates
Match candidate information
Identify professional contact information
Enrich candidate profiles
Verify candidate identity or professional information
Assist recruiters with candidate sourcing
Download or organize candidate resumes when requested
Present candidate information to the authorized user
Improve candidate search accuracy
Maintain user preferences and extension settings
Troubleshoot technical problems
Prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized access
Maintain and improve the MedHunt service
We do not use collected information to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
3. Chrome Extension Permissions
MedHunt may request Chrome permissions that are necessary to provide its candidate-sourcing functionality.
Depending on the version of the extension, these permissions may include:
Side Panel: Used to display MedHunt's recruiting interface alongside supported webpages.
Storage: Used to save extension preferences, workflow status, temporary data, and settings necessary for the extension to function.
Active Tab / Tabs: Used to identify and interact with the supported recruiting webpage that the user is actively using.
Scripting: Used to run MedHunt functionality on supported webpages during a user-initiated recruiting workflow.
Downloads: Used when a user requests that candidate resumes or recruiting-related files be downloaded.
Host Permissions: Used to interact with specifically supported recruiting websites required for MedHunt's candidate-sourcing functionality.
If the extension requests additional permissions, they are used only where reasonably necessary to provide MedHunt's disclosed recruiting functionality.
4. Website and Browsing Information
MedHunt is not designed to monitor a user's general browsing history.
The extension may identify the current webpage, URL, or page content when required to perform a user-requested action on a supported recruiting platform.
We do not use this information to create advertising profiles or track users across unrelated websites.
5. Data Enrichment and Third-Party Services
MedHunt may use third-party service providers to support candidate matching, contact discovery, data enrichment, hosting, infrastructure, or other functionality required to provide the service.
Where candidate information is submitted to a service provider, we limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary to perform the requested function.
Third-party providers may process information according to their own contractual obligations and privacy policies.
MedHunt does not authorize service providers to use information received through MedHunt for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.
6. Sale of Personal Information
MedHunt does not sell personal information collected through the extension.
We also do not transfer user data to third parties for unrelated advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or other purposes unrelated to MedHunt's recruiting functionality.
Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to provide the MedHunt service.
7. Health Information
Although MedHunt is designed for healthcare recruiting, professional healthcare information such as a person's occupation, specialty, license, certification, or employment history is used for recruiting purposes.
MedHunt is not designed to collect patient medical records, diagnoses, symptoms, treatment information, or other personal medical information.
Users should not submit protected health information or patient medical information to MedHunt.
8. Authentication and Passwords
MedHunt does not intentionally collect passwords or login credentials for third-party recruiting websites.
Users should never provide third-party passwords, security answers, PINs, or similar sensitive authentication credentials through MedHunt unless a feature specifically requires and securely supports such information.
9. Personal Communications
MedHunt is not designed to read or monitor a user's personal emails, private text messages, or unrelated communications.
If future functionality allows a user to send recruiting communications through MedHunt, such information will be used only to provide the requested recruiting functionality.
10. Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:
Provide MedHunt's services
Complete recruiting workflows
Maintain user accounts
Meet legitimate business requirements
Resolve disputes
Prevent fraud or abuse
Comply with applicable legal obligations
Temporary extension data may be deleted automatically when it is no longer required.
Users may contact us to request deletion of information where applicable.
11. Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.
These safeguards may include:
Secure network communications
Access controls
Authentication controls
Restricted internal access
Secure cloud infrastructure
Monitoring and logging
Encryption where appropriate
No electronic system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to maintain safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.
12. Limited Use of Data
Information collected or processed through MedHunt is used only for functionality related to MedHunt's disclosed purpose.
We do not use or transfer information for purposes unrelated to candidate sourcing, recruiting, staffing, candidate matching, contact discovery, or operating and securing the MedHunt service.
We do not use information for lending or creditworthiness decisions.
13. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
