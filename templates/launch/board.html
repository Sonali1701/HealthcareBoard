Capture candidates from anywhere

Install the browser extension to save candidates from job sites straight into your database — auto-deduplicated and owned by you.

  1. Download the extensionGet the packaged extension, pre-configured for your workspace.
  2. Load it in Chrome or EdgeUnzip it, open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Connect it to your accountOpen the extension's side panel and paste your connection token below so captures land under your account.
  4. Capture on a job siteOpen an Indeed results or profile page and click Capture — candidates flow into your central database.
Connection

Treat this token like a password — it lets the extension save candidates as you. Regenerating revokes the old one.
