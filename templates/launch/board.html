// Frequently asked

A few things to know.

Helpful details for healthcare professionals and staffing teams.

How complete are provider profiles?

Profile details vary. Confirm experience, availability and role requirements during your screening process.

Are all qualifications independently verified?

Use the appropriate credentialing channels to confirm license status and qualifications before hiring decisions.

Can agencies organize candidates and submissions?

Yes. MedHunt includes candidate pools and tools to manage submissions and placements.

What can agencies search by?

Search by name, credential, specialty, location and other available profile details. Filters help narrow results.

How can healthcare professionals use MedHunt?

Create an account to explore roles, maintain a professional profile and credentials, and manage applications and messages.

Capture candidates from anywhere

Install the browser extension to save candidates from job sites straight into your database — auto-deduplicated and owned by you.

  1. Download the extensionGet the packaged extension, pre-configured for your workspace.
  2. Load it in Chrome or EdgeUnzip it, open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Connect it to your accountOpen the extension's side panel and paste your connection token below so captures land under your account.
  4. Capture on a job siteOpen an Indeed results or profile page and click Capture — candidates flow into your central database.
Connection

Treat this token like a password — it lets the extension save candidates as you. Regenerating revokes the old one.
