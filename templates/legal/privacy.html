1. Who we are

HealthBoard is operated by [Company Legal Name] (“HealthBoard,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to the HealthBoard website and services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, password (stored hashed), role, and settings.
  • Clinician profile data — profession, specialty, work history, education, years of experience, location, pay preferences, availability, and NPI number.
  • Credentials — licenses and certifications, including type, state, number, status, and expiry dates.
  • Résumés and documents you upload, and information parsed from them.
  • Contact details — email and phone number (shown to recruiters only when released; see below).
  • Recruiter/agency data — organization details, job postings, talent pools, client records, and outreach content.
  • Messages you send through the Platform.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage & device data — log data, IP address, pages viewed, and actions taken, and cookie identifiers.

3. Where we get it

  • From you — when you register, build a profile, upload a résumé, post a job, or communicate.
  • Imported sources — some clinician profiles are created from résumés and from third-party applicant-tracking systems (such as LaborEdge Nexus) provided by agencies we work with. See Imported profiles.
  • Automatically — usage and device data collected as you use the Platform.

4. How we use it

  • Operate the marketplace — build profiles and directories, match Clinicians to jobs, and let Recruiters search and contact opted-in talent.
  • Provide features — messaging, credential tracking and expiry reminders, pay estimates, and analytics.
  • Process payments and manage credits.
  • Send transactional and service emails (for example, password resets, application updates, credential-expiry reminders, and recruiter messages you have chosen to receive).
  • Secure the Platform, prevent abuse and fraud, and enforce our terms.
  • Improve the Platform and comply with legal obligations.

5. How we share it

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information as follows:

  • With Recruiters — masked profile data appears in the directory; real name and contact details are shared only when released or when you apply (see below).
  • With service providers that help us operate, under contract, including: cloud hosting and database [Render / Neon], email delivery [SendGrid], payment processing [Stripe], AI features [LLM provider], and job-data sources such as LaborEdge Nexus.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and property of HealthBoard and others.
  • In a business transfer — as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Contact reveal & recruiters

A Clinician's name and contact details are masked in the directory by default. A Recruiter can reveal a Clinician's contact details by spending credits, or a Clinician's details become available to a specific employer when the Clinician applies to that employer's job. Once revealed, a Recruiter may contact the Clinician for genuine recruiting purposes. Recruiters are contractually prohibited from reselling or redistributing that information. Clinicians can control visibility and opt out at any time (see Your choices).

7. Imported profiles

Some profiles are created from résumés or applicant-tracking systems provided by agencies. If you are a clinician who did not create your own account, you still have rights over your data: you can request to see it, correct it, delist it from the directory, or delete it by contacting us at [privacy@yourdomain]. We honor these requests as described below and as required by law.

8. Your choices & rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. HealthBoard provides in-product controls for Clinicians to:

  • Delist — remove your profile from the searchable directory;
  • Export — download a copy of your data;
  • Delete — permanently delete your account and profile (with password confirmation), after which your contact details are scrubbed and your profile is removed from the directory.

You can also unsubscribe from non-transactional emails using the link in those emails. To exercise any right, use the in-product controls or contact [privacy@yourdomain]. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Because HealthBoard makes clinician information available to recruiters, certain disclosures may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under the CCPA/CPRA. 11. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Platform, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business records. Backups are purged on a rolling schedule.

12. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted transport, hashed passwords, access controls, and masking of contact details. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

The Platform is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
